Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

1. Introduction

AlmaVend respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide when visiting our website or contacting us about vending services.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide through our contact form, by email, by telephone, or through other direct communications with AlmaVend.

This information may include:

  • Your name;
  • Business or organization name;
  • Email address;
  • Telephone number;
  • Location or service address;
  • Information about your vending needs;
  • Any other information included in your message.

We may also receive limited technical information when you visit the website, such as browser type, device type, referring page, and general website activity.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Respond to questions and service inquiries;
  • Evaluate potential vending locations;
  • Recommend an appropriate vending solution;
  • Coordinate vending placement and ongoing service;
  • Communicate about appointments and service updates;
  • Maintain and improve our website and services;
  • Protect against fraud, misuse, or security issues;
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements.

4. SMS Communications

If you voluntarily provide your mobile telephone number and consent to receive text messages, AlmaVend may send messages related to your inquiry, location review, appointments, vending placement, and service updates.

Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchasing or receiving services. Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply.

You may reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for assistance.

Mobile information, text-message consent, and opt-in data will not be sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.

5. How We Share Information

AlmaVend does not sell your personal information.

We may share limited information with service providers that help us operate the website, communicate with customers, or provide requested services. These providers may use the information only for the services they perform for AlmaVend.

We may also disclose information when required by law, legal process, court order, or a valid governmental request, or when reasonably necessary to protect our rights, customers, business, or property.

6. Cookies and Website Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies that support basic website functionality, security, performance measurement, and visitor analytics.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect how some website features function.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries, provide requested services, maintain business records, resolve disputes, and satisfy legal obligations.

8. Information Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards intended to protect personal information. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for businesses and adults. AlmaVend does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Your Privacy Choices

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information that you have provided, subject to applicable legal and recordkeeping requirements.

You may also ask us to stop sending nonessential communications.

11. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. AlmaVend is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of websites operated by other organizations.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services, or privacy practices change. The current version will be identified by the “Last updated” date displayed at the top of this page.
